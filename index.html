Paragraph Structure Checker

Paste one paragraph to check its basic structure before you submit it. This tool checks structure only. It does not grade your ideas, and it will not rewrite your work.

What is being checked
  • Single paragraph: the text is one paragraph (complete / incomplete).
  • Three distinct quotations: at least three different quotations of three or more words each (complete / incomplete).
  • Topic sentence: the first sentence is a clear, declarative statement of your main claim.
  • Evidentiary claims: each quotation is tied to a specific claim and a named instance (a named author, text, provision, event, and so on).
  • Analysis after each quotation: the sentence right after a quotation states, in your own words, what the quotation shows. It does not restate it, treat it as self-evident, or carry on the argument without saying what the evidence establishes.
  • Negative framing: claims built on what something is not, lacks, or fails to do.
  • Negative parallelism: "not X but Y" constructions. Each is reported as "If not X, then Y" so you can state Y directly.
